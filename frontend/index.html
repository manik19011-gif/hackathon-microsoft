Frequently Asked Questions

Compliance & Technical Architecture

How does Azure OpenAI avoid hallucinations and false allegations?

Azure OpenAI is strictly bound to factual evidence generated by deterministic Python engines (exact line numbers, RapidFuzz similarity percentages, dates, and amounts). The LLM is never permitted to invent violations, extrapolate unprovided figures, or speculate outside the verified database state.

Why is RapidFuzz used instead of simple string matching?

Fraudulent and accidental duplicate invoices frequently alter subtle typographical elements (e.g. "Staples Inc" vs "STAPLES INCORPORATED" or "Dell Solutions LLC" vs "Dell Solutions"). RapidFuzz computes normalized token sort and token set ratios to catch deliberate punctuation or ordering shifts without generating false positives.

How does Benford's Law spot fabricated numbers?

In naturally occurring financial accounting distributions, the first digit '1' appears approximately 30.1% of the time, whereas '9' appears only 4.6% of the time. When bad actors invent fake receipts or inflate invoices, they disproportionately invent numbers starting with 4, 5, 7, or 9, producing high Mean Absolute Deviation (MAD) scores.

Is the audit trail compliant with SOX 404 requirements?

Yes. Every automated policy evaluation, single invoice check, file import, policy configuration change, and human auditor decision (Approve, Reject, Escalate) is permanently recorded with an immutable ISO 8601 timestamp and auditor identification in SQLite and exportable via CSV.
