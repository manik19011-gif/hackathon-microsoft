Multi-Layer Compliance Engine

Comprehensive Protection Against Corporate Expense Leakage

RapidFuzz Duplicate Matching

Detects exact duplicate invoice IDs alongside fuzzy vendor name variations (e.g. "Acme Supplies" vs "ACME Corp") with identical amounts.

Benford's Law Fraud Screening

Applies logarithmic leading-digit distribution frequency analysis to flag fabricated or manually altered invoice amounts.

Split Transaction Structuring

Catches multiple invoices submitted from the same vendor that intentionally hover just below policy approval thresholds (e.g. $4,900 to bypass $5,000 limits).

Azure OpenAI Evidence Generator

Translates compliance findings into human-readable audit explanations based strictly on factual evidence without hallucinating details.

Vendor Risk Intelligence

Ranks vendor risk profiles (HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW) based on violation frequency, spend volume, and historical non-compliance.

SOX Audit Trail Logs

Maintains an immutable audit log of all system ingestion events and human auditor decision actions (Approve, Reject, Escalate).
